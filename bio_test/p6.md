# Enzymes & Vitamins

## Enzymes
- **Biological catalysts** — mostly proteins
- **Highly specific** — one enzyme acts on one substrate
- **Increase reaction rate** without being consumed
- Names typically end with **-ase**

### Mechanism — Lock and Key Model
- Enzyme (lock) has a specific **active site**
- Substrate (key) fits precisely into the active site
- Forms **enzyme-substrate complex** → product + free enzyme

### Classification

| Class | Reaction Catalysed | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oxidoreductases | Oxidation-reduction | Dehydrogenase |
| Transferases | Group transfer | Transaminase |
| Hydrolases | Hydrolysis | Amylase, Lipase |
| Lyases | Addition/elimination | Fumarase |
| Isomerases | Isomerisation | Glucose isomerase |
| Ligases | Bond formation (ATP) | DNA ligase |

## Vitamins
- **Organic compounds** required in small amounts
- **Not synthesised** by body — must come from diet
- Classified as **fat-soluble** and **water-soluble**

| Vitamin | Solubility | Deficiency Disease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** (Retinol) | Fat | Night blindness |
| **B₁** (Thiamine) | Water | Beri-beri |
| **B₂** (Riboflavin) | Water | Cheilosis |
| **B₆** (Pyridoxine) | Water | Anaemia |
| **B₁₂** (Cyanocobalamin) | Water | Pernicious anaemia |
| **C** (Ascorbic acid) | Water | Scurvy |
| **D** (Calciferol) | Fat | Rickets |
| **E** (Tocopherol) | Fat | Sterility |
| **K** (Phylloquinone) | Fat | Delayed clotting |
