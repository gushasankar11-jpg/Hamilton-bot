# Peptides, Proteins & Their Structure

## Peptide Bond
- Formed between **-COOH** of one amino acid and **-NH₂** of another
- Loss of **H₂O** molecule (condensation)
- Resulting -CO-NH- linkage is a **peptide bond**

[DIAGRAM: peptide_bond]

### Peptide Terminology
- **Dipeptide**: 2 amino acids
- **Tripeptide**: 3 amino acids
- **Oligopeptide**: few (2–10)
- **Polypeptide**: many (>10)
- **Protein**: polypeptide with **>100** amino acids

## Structure of Proteins

### Primary Structure
- **Sequence** of amino acids in the polypeptide chain
- Determines all higher structures

### Secondary Structure
- Regular **local folding** of the backbone
- Two types:
  - **α-Helix**: right-handed spiral, H-bonds within chain

[DIAGRAM: alpha_helix]

  - **β-Pleated Sheet**: side-by-side chains, H-bonds between chains

[DIAGRAM: beta_sheet]

### Tertiary Structure
- **3D shape** of the entire polypeptide
- Stabilised by disulfide bridges, H-bonds, hydrophobic interactions

### Quaternary Structure
- **Assembly of multiple polypeptide subunits**
- Example: **Haemoglobin** (4 subunits: 2α + 2β)

## Denaturation of Proteins
- Loss of biological activity due to **loss of native structure**
- Caused by: heat, pH change, urea, heavy metals
- **Primary structure is NOT destroyed** — only 2°/3°/4° structure
- Example: coagulation of egg white on boiling
