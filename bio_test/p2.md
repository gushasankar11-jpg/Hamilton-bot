# Glucose — Structure & Reactions

## Structure of Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)
- **Open chain** (Fischer) structure — has an aldehyde group at C1

- **Cyclic** (Haworth) structure — exists as a 6-membered ring (pyranose)

## Reactions of Glucose

| Reagent | Observation | Inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HI** (conc.) | n-Hexane formed | All 6 C in straight chain |
| **HNO₃** | Saccharic acid | Aldehyde + primary OH present |
| **Br₂ water** | Gluconic acid | Aldehyde group present (mild) |
| **HCN** | Cyanohydrin | Aldehyde group present |
| **Tollens / Fehling** | Positive (red ppt) | **Reducing sugar** |
| **Acetic anhydride** | Penta-acetate | 5 OH groups |
| **CH₃OH / HCl** | Methyl glucoside | Cyclic structure confirmed |

## Anomers & Mutarotation
- **Anomers**: α-D-glucose and β-D-glucose differ at C1 (anomeric carbon)
- **Mutarotation**: spontaneous change in optical rotation of fresh glucose solution
- Equilibrium mixture: **36% α + 64% β**

## Pyranose vs Furanose
- **Pyranose** = 6-membered ring (glucose)
- **Furanose** = 5-membered ring (fructose)
