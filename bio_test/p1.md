# Biomolecules — Introduction & Carbohydrates

## What are Biomolecules?
- **Biomolecules** are organic molecules that form the basis of life
- They are the **building blocks** of living organisms
- Major classes: **Carbohydrates, Proteins, Enzymes, Vitamins, Nucleic Acids, Hormones**

## Carbohydrates
- **Definition**: Polyhydroxy aldehydes or ketones (or compounds which yield them on hydrolysis)
- General formula: **Cₙ(H₂O)ₘ** (hence "hydrates of carbon")
- **Source of energy** — main fuel for the body
- Produced by plants during **photosynthesis**

## Classification of Carbohydrates

| Class | Example | Hydrolysis Product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monosaccharides | Glucose, Fructose | Cannot be hydrolysed further |
| Disaccharides | Sucrose, Maltose, Lactose | 2 monosaccharide units |
| Polysaccharides | Starch, Cellulose, Glycogen | Many monosaccharide units |

## Monosaccharides — Key Features
- Simplest carbohydrates, **cannot be hydrolysed**
- Sweet in taste, soluble in water
- Contain **3–7 carbon atoms**
  - Triose (3C): Glyceraldehyde
  - Tetrose (4C): Erythrose
  - Pentose (5C): Ribose, Deoxyribose
  - Hexose (6C): **Glucose, Fructose**
- Free aldehyde/ketone group present
