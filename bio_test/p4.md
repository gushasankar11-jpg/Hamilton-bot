# Polysaccharides & Amino Acids

## Polysaccharides

| Polysaccharide | Monomer | Linkage | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starch** | α-D-Glucose | α-1,4 & α-1,6 | Energy storage (plants) |
| **Cellulose** | β-D-Glucose | β-1,4 | Structural (plant cell wall) |
| **Glycogen** | α-D-Glucose | α-1,4 & α-1,6 | Energy storage (animals) |

### Key Differences
- **Starch**: mixture of **amylose** (linear, α-1,4) + **amylopectin** (branched, α-1,6)
- **Cellulose**: **linear** β-1,4 — humans cannot digest (no cellulase enzyme)
- **Glycogen**: more branched than amylopectin — "animal starch"

## Amino Acids — Building Blocks of Proteins
- Contain both **amino group (-NH₂)** and **carboxyl group (-COOH)**
- **α-amino acids** are the natural ones — NH₂ and COOH on same carbon
- General structure: **H₂N — CH(R) — COOH**

### Zwitterion (Isoelectric form)
- In aqueous solution, amino acids exist as **dipolar ions**
- **NH₃⁺ — CH(R) — COO⁻**
- Has both positive and negative charge → neutral overall
- This is why amino acids have **high melting points** and are water-soluble

### Classification of Amino Acids

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Essential** (must be in diet) | Valine, Leucine, Isoleucine, Lysine |
| **Non-essential** (body makes them) | Glycine, Alanine, Serine |
| **Acidic** | Aspartic acid, Glutamic acid |
| **Basic** | Lysine, Arginine, Histidine |
| **Neutral** | Glycine, Alanine, Valine |
