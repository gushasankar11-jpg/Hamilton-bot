# Fructose & Disaccharides

## Fructose (C₆H₁₂O₆)
- **Ketohexose** — contains a ketone group at C2
- Sweetest natural sugar ("fruit sugar")
- Structure: open chain + cyclic (furanose form)

## Disaccharides

| Disaccharide | Composition | Glycosidic Link | Reducing? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sucrose** | Glucose + Fructose | α1 → β2 | **Non-reducing** |
| **Maltose** | Glucose + Glucose | α1 → 4 | Reducing |
| **Lactose** | Galactose + Glucose | β1 → 4 | Reducing |

### Sucrose — Special Points
- **Non-reducing** because both anomeric carbons are involved in the glycosidic bond
- Does **not** reduce Tollens/Fehling reagent
- On hydrolysis gives **equimolar** glucose + fructose (called **invert sugar**)
- Hydrolysis by **invertase** enzyme

### Maltose
- Also called **malt sugar**
- Two α-D-glucose units joined by α-1,4-glycosidic bond
- Reducing sugar

### Lactose
- **Milk sugar**
- Reduces Tollens reagent
- Composed of β-D-galactose + β-D-glucose
