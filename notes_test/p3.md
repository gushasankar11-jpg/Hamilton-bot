## Energy, Momentum and Pressure

### Energy Density
The total energy per unit volume:

$u = \frac{1}{2}\epsilon_0 E^2 + \frac{1}{2\mu_0} B^2$

Averaged over time:

$\langle u \rangle = \epsilon_0 E_0^2 = \frac{B_0^2}{\mu_0}$

### Poynting Vector
Represents energy flow per unit area per unit time:

$S = \frac{1}{\mu_0} E \times B$

Magnitude: $|S| = u c$

### Radiation Pressure
- Complete absorption: $P = I/c$
- Complete reflection: $P = 2I/c$
