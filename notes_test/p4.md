## Electromagnetic Spectrum

[DIAGRAM: em_spectrum]

### Arranged by Increasing Wavelength
γ-rays, X-rays, UV, Visible, IR, Microwaves, Radio waves

### Key Facts
- All EM waves travel with the same speed $c$ in vacuum
- They differ only in **wavelength (or frequency) and energy**
- Energy of a photon: $E = h\nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$
- Planck's constant: $h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34} \, J \cdot s$

### Order of Increasing Energy
Radio < Microwave < IR < Visible < UV < X-rays < γ-rays
