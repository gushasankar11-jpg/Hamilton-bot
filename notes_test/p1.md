# Electromagnetic Waves — Introduction

## What are EM waves?
- Produced by **accelerated charges**
- Consist of oscillating **E** and **B** fields
- **E ⊥ B ⊥ direction of propagation** (mutually perpendicular)
- Transverse in nature
- Do **not** require a material medium

## Basic Properties
- Carry energy and momentum
- Exert **radiation pressure**
- Obey superposition, reflection, refraction, interference
- Speed in vacuum: $c = 3 \times 10^8 \, m/s$
- In a medium with refractive index $n$: $v = c/n$
