## Wave Equations in Vacuum

For a plane EM wave travelling along +x:

$E = E_0 \sin(kx - \omega t)$

$B = B_0 \sin(kx - \omega t)$

Both **E** and **B** are **in phase** — they reach maxima and minima together.

### Important Relations
- $E_0 = c B_0$
- $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}$
- $c = \lambda \nu = \omega / k$

Where:
- $E_0$ = max electric field
- $B_0$ = max magnetic field
- $\lambda$ = wavelength
- $\nu$ = frequency
- $\omega = 2\pi\nu$ = angular frequency
